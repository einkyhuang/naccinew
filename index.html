Assurance & Knowledge

5-Year Warranty Statement & FAQ

Transparent warranties and answers to essential technical inquiries regarding battery safety, cycle life, low temperature operation, and charging guidelines.

How safe are NACCI Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) Batteries? ▼
Our LiFePO4 batteries are non-flammable, non-combustible, and non-hazardous due to their superior olivine crystal chemical structure. When subjected to hazardous collisions or external short-circuiting, they will not explode or release toxic gases, significantly protecting human safety.
What is a BMS, what does it do, and where is it located? ▼
BMS stands for Battery Management System. It acts as the intelligent guardian for every cell inside the pack, protecting against over-voltage, under-voltage, over-current, and extreme temperature conditions. Our systems feature Over-the-Air (OTA) remote telemetry and intelligent cell balancing.
What is the expected design life and cycle life of NACCI batteries? ▼
NACCI batteries are rated for 3,500 to 6,000 deep cycles. The overall design life is 10 to 15 years, backed by our 5-year replacement warranty. Upgrading from lead-acid saves up to 70% in total battery expenditure over 5 years.
Can NACCI batteries operate in freezing winter conditions? ▼
Yes. Our standard packs discharge reliably down to -20°C (-4°F). With our optional built-in PTC self-heating technology, the battery automatically preheats cells before charging in freezing sub-zero environments, preventing lithium dendrite formation.
Can lithium batteries be charged anytime (Opportunity Charging)? ▼
Absolutely. Unlike lead-acid batteries which suffer from memory effect and require rigid 8-hour uninterrupted charge cycles, LiFePO4 batteries thrive on opportunity charging. Plug them in during lunch breaks or shift pauses to keep equipment running 24/7 without degrading cell life.
